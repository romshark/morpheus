neo-compare: dragging large HTML layers

Each drag move used to write an inherited custom property on the shadow root of <neo-compare>, which made every slotted node restyle. At 10000 spans a move took 2.9 ms in Chrome and 36 ms in WebKit. The position now goes in --_neo-compare-v, registered with inherits: false and written on the 4 elements that draw the divider. A move should stay under 0.2 ms at any size.

  1. Pick a number of slotted spans.
  2. Click Measure, or drag the divider and read the frame times.

A frame lasts 16.7 ms at 60 Hz and 8.3 ms at 120 Hz. A frame time above that means the drag dropped frames.
