neo-elastic: collapsed content and focus

<neo-elastic open="false"> collapses to height: 0 with overflow: hidden and makes its content inert. Without inert, the link, the input and the button inside stay in the Tab order and in the accessibility tree, with no visible area for a focus ring.

  1. Click Before.
  2. Press Tab.

Expected: focus moves to After. With Toggle open expanded, it moves to the link instead. The status turns red if focus lands inside the collapsed element.
