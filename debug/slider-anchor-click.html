neo-slider: off-step anchor click

Both sliders have step="10" and marks at 25, 50 and 75. A click on the 25 or 75 dot used to commit 30 or 80, the nearest step. #onTrackPointerDown sets pointer capture on the track, which makes the browser fire the click at the track, not the dot, and the anchor click handler never ran. The press now commits the dot's value. Marks are also stops on the step grid, unless static-marks is set.

  1. Click the 25 dot and the 75 dot on each slider without moving the pointer. Each row should read PASS. The 50 mark is on the step grid and passes either way.
  2. Focus the neo-slider thumb, press Home, then ArrowRight 4 times. The value goes 10, 20, 25, 30.
  3. Click the rail just right of the 25 dot. The value is 25.

click target in the log shows where the browser sent the click. A press that moves 4px or more is a drag and isn't judged.

Synthetic events can't reproduce this, because setPointerCapture throws for a pointer the browser doesn't track, and the click then reaches the dot.
