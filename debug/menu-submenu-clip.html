neo-menu: submenu cut off in Safari

Safari drew a submenu cut off for a split second as it opened, when its panel was drawn inside a transformed <neo-button>. Any transform, even translateY(0), makes the button the containing block and stacking context of the fixed menu panels. A <neo-button> that holds a <neo-menu> now has no transform.

  1. Click File, hover Settings, then move between Editor and Theme.
  2. Expected: the Theme submenu renders whole every time.
